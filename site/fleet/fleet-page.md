# The fleet page

The fleet page shows everyone in your fleet, live. It's on your hub's address in any browser or phone, and inside Starglance: **Fleet** in the top bar opens it in the same window.

## The roster

On the left, everyone, grouped your way:

- **Ship**: crews together under their ship, with everyone's seat. People on foot aboard a ship show with its crew.
- **Server**: who you can actually meet in game.
- **Status**: in a ship, on foot, not in game, offline.
- **A–Z**.

Each row says where someone is ("Pilot · Lorville") and has the things that matter at a glance: on your server, in an armistice zone, a warning, NAV/SCM/QT, fuel and health bars. Click anyone to open their dashboard.

## Overview

Session totals (kills, contracts, quantum trips, aUEC earned, ships lost, downed), the ships out with their crews, and everyone's activity as it happens, with filters.

![Overview](/shots/fleet-overview.jpg)

## Cards

Everyone's numbers side by side, for flying together: speed on a small arc, altitude, climb rate, heading and G, with fuel and health as rings, and the quantum destination in a ship; health, oxygen, food and water as rings, and injuries, on foot. Drag a card to reorder; **Hide offline** keeps it tidy.

After a quantum jump, a card shows where it ended, dimmed: **Arrived Bounty Beacon · 6m ago**. That's where they arrived, not necessarily where they are now: the game logs arriving, but not leaving. It goes away when they plot a new route, change server or die, or after 20 minutes.

![Cards](/shots/fleet-cards.jpg)

## Anyone's dashboard

Click a name to open their dashboard: the same panels as your own cockpit view, in the theme of the ship they're flying if they have **Match the ship** on.

![A member's dashboard](/shots/fleet-member.jpg)

## Fleet alarms

The top bar shows anyone's master warning, or a bad injury, as it happens. Click it to go straight to their screen. Which lights count is up to you: **Settings → Header alerts**.

On a big screen (1440p, 4K) the page grows to fill it, everything in proportion, the same as the cockpit view; on a phone the roster is the page and a member opens full screen. **Settings → Look → Size** picks a fixed size instead.

## Wingmates

The **Wingmates** panel puts the fleet in your own cockpit view: everyone's ship, mode, speed and altitude next to your own numbers. It's in the Pilot and Combat layouts, or add it in **Settings → Panels**.

## On a phone

The roster is the page; tap someone to open them full screen. Sign your phone in from **Your account → Sign in on another device** ([Joining a fleet](/fleet/joining#another-pc-or-your-phone)).

<img src="/shots/fleet-phone.jpg" alt="The fleet page on a phone" style="max-width: 320px">

**Add it to your Home Screen** to open it like an app, full screen with the Starglance icon: on iPhone, **Share → Add to Home Screen**; on Android, **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) in Chrome's menu. If it asks you to sign in, use **Your account → Sign in on another device** and type the code in the app. On iPhone, keeping the screen awake needs this (see [Tablets, phones and VR](/guide/other-devices#your-phone-anywhere)).

## Inside Starglance

**Fleet** in the cockpit view's top bar shows the fleet in the same window, through your PC's link: no separate sign-in. **Open on the hub** there, or `starglance open`, opens the hub's page in your browser, signed in.
